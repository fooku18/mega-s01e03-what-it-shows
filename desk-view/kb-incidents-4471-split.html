How each report was placed

  1. One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. Count each id once.
  2. By tenant, not by browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts). Every tenant on file writes the day first. The intake tag comes from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong: sort by tenant and form.
  3. hint: the report came in through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first, and the form reads the date day first. Fixing the shared parser does not touch this.
  4. parser: every other report from a day-first tenant. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month flip.
  5. One-day move (desk-notes.json): these dates are not flipped. They are one day off, or differ between two people for the same meeting (Lisbon and Toronto). That points at a time zone or midnight bug, not at date order.

Lessons

  • Same symptom is not same cause. “Wrong date” came from three bugs; place reports by tenant and channel before you merge them.
  • Read a sample of bodies before you mark a big ticket known.
