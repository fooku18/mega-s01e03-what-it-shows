How each report was placed

  1. One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. Count each id once.
  2. By tenant, not by browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts). Every tenant on file writes the day first.
  3. hint: the report came in through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first, and the form reads the date day first. Fixing the shared parser does not touch this.
  4. parser: every other report from a day-first tenant. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month flip.
  5. one-day: the reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). These dates are not flipped; they are one day off, or differ between two people for the same meeting. That points at a time zone or midnight bug, not at date order.

The intake tag is a guess

Intake tagged 41 reports hint; 23 of them are not. Of the 33 reports that are hint, intake tagged 15 as parser.

The tag comes from the reporter’s browser at intake. It is not the cause: sort by tenant and form.
